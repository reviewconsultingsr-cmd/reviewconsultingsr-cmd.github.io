Stuck on your topic, methodology or statistics? I guide you step by step so your thesis or dissertation is approved by your supervisor and committee, without losing months.

Where students usually need help

Most master's theses in health sciences, psychology and the social sciences are based on a review of the literature or a small quantitative study. The difficult points are almost always the same: a question that is too broad, a methodology that is not described properly and a statistical analysis that does not answer the question.

If your thesis is a systematic review or includes a meta-analysis, see those pages as well.

Frequently asked questions

Will you write my thesis for me?

No. I provide methodological guidance and feedback so the work is yours and meets your institution's academic integrity rules. That way you can defend it with confidence in front of your committee.

Which fields can you help with?

Mainly medicine, nursing, health sciences and psychology, but also education, social sciences, business and maritime studies, when the work is based on a review or quantitative research.

Do you also help with PhD dissertations?

Yes, especially with systematic reviews, meta-analyses and statistical analysis for chapters or publications of the dissertation.

How does the collaboration work?

Online, with video calls and written comments on your files, so you can work on your own schedule from anywhere.

My deadline is in a few weeks. Is it still possible?

Often yes, if the plan is realistic. Write your deadline in the assessment form and I will tell you honestly what can be done.

How much does it cost?

It depends on the scope of the work and the stages you need help with. After the free assessment you receive a clear quote before any work begins.
