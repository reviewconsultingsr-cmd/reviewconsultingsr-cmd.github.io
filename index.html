How a review is built

Five stages, each documented so your supervisor, the editor and the reviewers can follow every decision.

  1. Question and protocol

    PICO or PCC, eligibility criteria and a protocol registered in PROSPERO or OSF.

  2. Search strategy

    Database-specific searches with MeSH terms and Boolean logic, recorded so anyone can repeat them.

  3. Screening and extraction

    Two independent reviewers, a PRISMA flow diagram and a piloted extraction form.

  4. Analysis and certainty

    Risk of bias with validated tools, synthesis or meta-analysis, and GRADE.

  5. Manuscript and submission

    PRISMA-compliant reporting, journal selection and responses to reviewers.

Frequently asked questions

If your question isn't here, just ask in the form.

Do you help with PhD, master's and bachelor's theses?

Yes. We provide methodological support for doctoral theses, master's and bachelor's theses and any other university project: the research question, the proposal, the literature review, the methods, statistics in R or SPSS, the writing and preparation for the defence. The work remains yours.

Which subjects and disciplines do you cover?

All of them. We support university assignments and projects in every discipline, from medicine, nursing and psychology to education, social sciences, economics, business, law, engineering and the humanities. The work remains yours.

Which type of review do I need?

It depends on your question. A literature review explains a topic or builds an argument, a scoping review maps all the evidence on a topic, and a systematic review answers a focused question, often with a meta-analysis. The free assessment includes a recommendation.

Is the first assessment really free?

Yes. You receive confidential feedback within 48 hours, with no obligation to continue.

Do you write the review for me?

I provide methodological support, training and expert input so that the work remains yours and meets your institution's rules on academic integrity. The scope is agreed with you in advance.

Is my project kept confidential?

Yes. Your topic, data and drafts are never shared with anyone else.

Can you help in Greek?

Yes. Support is available in English and Greek.
