PhD thesis support: methodology, systematic reviews and statistics for your doctorate

A doctorate is a long project with many moving parts: the proposal, a literature review that stands up to scrutiny, publications along the way and a defence at the end. I support PhD candidates with the methodological parts, so you keep moving and the work stays yours.

Where PhD candidates usually get stuck

Most doctoral candidates are strong in their subject but have had little formal training in research methods. The same problems come up again and again: a proposal that the committee sends back, a literature review that is broad but not systematic, data that are hard to analyse, and articles that are rejected for methodological reasons.

Many universities now expect a systematic review as the first chapter or the first publication of a PhD, often with a meta-analysis. Done well, it gives you a solid base for the rest of your doctorate and an early publication.

Support at every stage of your doctorate

You can ask for help with one stage or with the whole journey.

Research proposal

A clear research question, aims and objectives, a feasible design and a methods section your committee will approve.

Systematic review as your first chapter

Protocol, PROSPERO registration, database searches, screening, risk of bias and PRISMA 2020 reporting.

Study design and data collection

Sample size, questionnaires and validated instruments, ethics applications and a data management plan.

Statistical analysis

Analysis in R or SPSS, from descriptive statistics to regression models and meta-analysis, explained so you can defend every number.

Publications from your thesis

Turning chapters into journal articles, choosing the right journal and responding to reviewers.

Thesis writing and defence

Structure, consistency between chapters, APA 7 references and preparation for your viva.

Frequently asked questions

Will you write my PhD thesis for me?

No. I provide methodological guidance, training and expert feedback so the thesis is yours and meets your university's academic integrity rules. That is also what lets you defend it with confidence.

Can you help with only one chapter or one article?

Yes. Many candidates ask for help with a single stage, for example the systematic review chapter or the statistical analysis of one study.

Which software do you use for statistics?

R and SPSS. You receive the code or the syntax and a plain-language explanation of every result.

Do you work with candidates outside Greece?

Yes. Everything happens online, with clients worldwide. We work in English or Greek.
